Privacy Policy

Effective May 12, 2026

Summary

FOWCUS is a personal focus tool. We collect the minimum data needed to make the app work. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not show you ads. We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or use-based data mining.

What we collect

  • Your Apple ID identifier (returned by Sign in with Apple). If you choose to share your email, we store it so you can recover your account.
  • Your FOWCUS settings: the opaque token your device returns from Apple's Family Activity Picker, your daily goal, challenge difficulty, and optional always-block schedules.
  • A log of your focus sessions and challenge attempts (start time, duration, completion, whether you walked away) so we can show your streak and weekly recap.
  • Your subscription status, retrieved from RevenueCat using your anonymous identifier.

What we do NOT collect

  • The names of the apps you block. iOS gives FOWCUS an opaque identifier (a "token") for each app you pick. The token is not a name, a bundle ID, or anything we can decode back into an app name.
  • Usage of the apps you block. We never see when, how often, or for how long you use Instagram, TikTok, or any other app — iOS does not expose that to FOWCUS.
  • Your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or browsing history.
  • Advertising or tracking identifiers. We do not use the App Tracking Transparency framework because we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Camera

The breathing exercise can optionally show your face on screen during the 20-second breathing portion of the unlock challenge. The camera feed is rendered locally on your device and is never recorded, transmitted, or stored. If you deny camera access, the breathing exercise still works — you just see the breathing circle alone.

Where data is stored

  • On your device, in iOS-managed storage and in a shared App Group accessible to FOWCUS and its DeviceActivityMonitor extension.
  • On our server, hosted by Supabase (Postgres) in encrypted form, protected by row-level security policies so only you can read your own rows.
  • Subscription state is managed by RevenueCat and Apple's App Store.

How we use the data

We use the data only to operate FOWCUS: to keep the shield in sync, to compute your streak, to show your stats, to fire notifications you ask for, and to gate access to the app behind your subscription. We do not use Family Controls / Screen Time data for advertising, marketing, or use-based data mining, as required by Apple's developer agreement.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with the following service providers, who act on our behalf to deliver the app:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, App Store, Family Controls APIs.
  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting.
  • RevenueCat — subscription receipt validation.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account is scheduled for permanent deletion 30 days after the request; signing back in within that window cancels the deletion. After 30 days, every row tied to your account is removed from our database. Deleting your account does not cancel your App Store subscription — manage that in the App Store under Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

To request a copy of your data or to ask any privacy question, email adamlcpro44@gmail.com.

Children

FOWCUS is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. The Family Controls authorization scope FOWCUS requests is the adult .individual scope, never the parental .child scope.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and surface the new version in the app the next time you open it.
