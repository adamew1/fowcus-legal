What FOWCUS does

FOWCUS is a personal focus tool. It uses Apple's Screen Time / Family Controls APIs to block apps you choose, on the device you install it on. To use a blocked app, you complete a friction challenge that unlocks all your blocked apps together for a duration you pick. When that duration ends, the shield reapplies automatically.
